About CampusBuzz

CampusBuzz is a college event management website designed to make it easier for students to discover, explore and register for college events.

From cultural festivals and technical events to sports activities and competitions, CampusBuzz brings campus activities together in one place.

🎯 Our Goal

To make college events easy to discover and participate in.

🎉 Student Experience

Students can explore events and keep track of their registrations.

📱 Easy to Use

A simple and user-friendly platform for college students.
